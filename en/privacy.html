Privacy Policy

ScreenToCar (iOS and Android) · Effective October 1, 2026

Summary: ScreenToCar does not collect personal data.

There are no servers, accounts, or analytics. Your screen travels only over the local network (such as Personal Hotspot) between your iPhone and your car's browser. It is never sent elsewhere or stored.

1. Information we handle

  • Screen video: encoded on your iPhone only while screen sharing is on, and sent directly to the car's browser on the same local network. It is not recorded or saved.
  • App settings (such as quality) and purchase status: stored only on your iPhone.
  • Purchases: handled by the Apple App Store. The app checks only Apple-signed purchase records on your device. The app never receives payment information.

1-1. Location (safety limit while driving)

Only while screen sharing is on, the app checks your speed on the device. If you're driving, it automatically limits the car display to navigation mode (a few frames per second). Location is never stored, never leaves your device, and is used for nothing else. If you don't allow location access, sharing still works but always uses navigation mode.

2. Sharing with third parties

None. The app uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or payment SDKs.

3. Local network permission

The app asks for local network access so your car's browser can connect to your iPhone and receive the screen. It is used for nothing else.

4. Deletion

Deleting the app removes all settings stored on your iPhone. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in iPhone Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

5. Contact

ScreenToCar is an independent app, not affiliated with Tesla. Contact: support@dawonlabs.com
